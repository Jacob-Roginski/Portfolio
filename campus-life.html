Product design · Fall 2025

Campus Life

A calmer way for students to find their place on campus.

Campus Life brings events, campus resources, community updates, and personalized recommendations into one approachable mobile experience.

RoleProduct Designer ToolsFigma · Prototyping FocusDiscovery · UX · UI

See it in motion

From first login to finding your next thing.

The opportunity

Campus information is everywhere. The student experience should not be.

Students often have to move between calendars, group chats, campus websites, and scattered announcements just to decide what to do next. Campus Life turns that fragmented search into a single, personalized starting point.

01

Find what fits

Recommendations and filters help students discover events and communities that match their interests.

02

Stay in the loop

A focused feed makes important updates visible without creating another noisy stream.

03

Feel oriented

Clear navigation, helpful context, and a guided setup make campus feel easier to navigate.

The design system

Friendly, useful, and built for quick decisions.

The visual system uses a warm green palette, rounded cards, and short content blocks to make the app feel welcoming without competing with the information itself.

  • Personalized onboarding that asks only what matters
  • Consistent card patterns for events, rules, and updates
  • Persistent bottom navigation for fast, familiar access
  • Accessible contrast and generous touch targets

Reflection

Designing for belonging starts with reducing friction.

This project reinforced how much a product’s tone is shaped by the small moments: a clear first question, a useful recommendation, or the confidence that comes from knowing where to go next. Campus Life is designed to make those moments feel simple and encouraging.
